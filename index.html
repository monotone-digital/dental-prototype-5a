FAQ

Everything you need to know before your visit

Still have a question?0322 6375789
Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

Both are fine. Booking ahead by phone or WhatsApp means less waiting, but we'll always try to see walk-in patients, especially if you're in pain.

Is root canal treatment painful?

Modern root canal treatment is done under local anaesthesia and feels much like a filling. Most patients are surprised how comfortable it is, and it relieves the toothache.

How often should I have a check-up?

Every six months is ideal for most adults and children. Regular check-ups catch small problems early, when treatment is simpler and cheaper.

At what age should my child first see a dentist?

Around their first birthday, or when the first teeth appear. Early, friendly visits help children feel relaxed about dental care for life.

Will I know the cost before treatment starts?

Yes. After your examination we explain every option and its cost clearly, and nothing goes ahead until you're happy with the plan.
